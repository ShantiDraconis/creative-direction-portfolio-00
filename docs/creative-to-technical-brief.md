# Creative-to-technical brief — original example

**Illustrative concept, not a commissioned production.**

## Concept
A three-scene live performance exploring transition from darkness to connection through lighting, spatial composition and original audio.

## Cue intent
| Cue | Dramaturgy | Lighting | Audio | Stage | Safety / fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Arrival | Low-level side light | Original ambient bed | Clear entrance | Manual scene preset |
| C02 | Transition | Gradual color-temperature shift | Rhythmic rise | Controlled reposition | Hold on current cue |
| C03 | Resolution | Full-stage reveal | Original ending motif | Final position | House-light recovery |

## Technical direction workflow
Creative brief → feasibility review → cue sheet → programming → rehearsal notes → revision approval → performance → debrief.

## Management behaviours
Communicate observable facts, maintain role boundaries, document changes, solicit technical feedback and escalate safety risks. Personal adversity is not evidence of a clinical or leadership qualification.
