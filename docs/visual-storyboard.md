# Visual storyboard — three-scene concept

**Textual visual specification; no production photographs claimed.**

| Frame | Composition | Lighting language | Audience intention |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single silhouette, negative space | Narrow side key, low intensity | Focus and anticipation |
| 2 | Moving ensemble, layered depth | Gradual crossfade and backlight | Movement and tension |
| 3 | Open stage, balanced symmetry | Broad wash with controlled highlights | Release and clarity |
