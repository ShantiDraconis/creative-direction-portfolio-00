# Leadership, feedback and conflict-management framework

This framework translates practical lessons from high-pressure production environments into **professional behaviours**, without disclosing private experiences, psychological history or unverified allegations.

1. **Observe:** Record what happened, when, and the operational impact.
2. **Separate interpretation:** Do not infer motives from tone, facial expressions or rumours.
3. **Communicate:** Use a concise problem statement, risk level and proposed next step.
4. **Clarify authority:** Identify the person accountable for go/no-go and safety decisions.
5. **Document:** Keep a neutral handover record, avoiding personal judgements.
6. **Repair:** Re-test the technical issue and seek feedback on the process.
7. **Improve:** Update the checklist or training material to prevent recurrence.

Evidence for a management application should come from demonstrable coordination, training, procedures and results, not from the existence of trauma or conflict.
